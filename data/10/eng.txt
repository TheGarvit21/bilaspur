Bik Chand or Bikram Chand, Gyan Chand's son from his Hindu wife, won his inheritance by battle with his Muslim brothers. Bikram Chand went to Kot-Kehloor to be installed as the Raja, leaving behind his two Ranis at Sunhani. One of these Ranis was from Kangra and the other from Baghal. A son was born to the Kangra Rani, but she delayed in passing on the news to the Raja. The news of the other Rani's son who took birth almost at the same time, arrived first. On learning this, the Kangra Rani at once claimed priority for her son, who on full enquiry, was recognized as the heir-apparent. His name was Sultan Chand, whereas the name of the other son was Keshab Chand. Keshab Chand continued staying at Sunhani and founded Keshab Nagar at village Panol, which was later destroyed in a fire.
